About

Who you'd work with

I founded Aequum Labs when I went independent in 2026.

I've spent more than 20 years in pharma marketing, from three sides of the table: brand management at Pfizer, commercial strategy and analytics at WebMD/Medscape, and omnichannel experience planning at Omnicom healthcare agencies. The work spans launches and loss of exclusivity, HCP and patient programs, and therapeutic areas from oncology and cardiovascular to HIV, rare disease, and multiple sclerosis.
