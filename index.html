Why “Aequum”?

A nod to balance, fairness, and level ground.

Aequum draws from Latin roots — related to aequus — often associated with what is level, balanced, or fair. It suggests a kind of evenness: not tilted in favor of one side, not overloaded, not chaotic.

That idea resonates with what we want our products to do. The goal isn’t to chase every trend, but to create software that helps everyday work feel more balanced: clearer workloads, fewer surprises, and tools that quietly support the people using them.

Software projects & explorations

Alongside Callbook, a portfolio of open-source tools and software ideas is taking shape.

Open Source Projects

Svelo

Svelo is an open‑source cryptography educational tool that demystifies encoding and decoding. Built as a lightweight command‑line interface, it automatically tries common ciphers and encodings on any input string, helping users learn about Base64, hex, ROT13, and dozens of other encoding schemes through hands‑on experimentation.

PIDpal

PIDpal is an open‑source process explainer that bridges the gap between raw system data and human understanding. By providing plain‑English explanations of running processes on Linux, macOS, and Windows, it helps anyone—from curious users to system administrators—learn what's happening on their computer without parsing cryptic technical output.

Product concepts & projects

Project AQ-ProjectManager

Project AQ-ProjectManager is an internal goals‑and‑execution workspace that keeps goals, projects, and tasks in one place. It combines a board‑style view of work with structured Markdown summaries, making it easier to review progress or feed updates into large language models.

Project AQ-PortfolioManager

Project AQ-PortfolioManager is a flexible content management system for cataloging and presenting works of art online. It combines AI‑powered metadata generation with thoughtful workflow tools for review, approval, and multi‑platform distribution, making it easier for artists and institutions to organize collections and share them with audiences across different channels.

Project AQ-InteractiveResume

Project AQ-InteractiveResume helps professionals audit and organize their career history into an interactive web experience. Instead of static résumés, it lets site visitors filter achievements and projects by role type or custom criteria, surfacing the most relevant experience for the position they're hiring for—turning decades of work into a browsable, context‑aware story.
