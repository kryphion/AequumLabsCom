About

Who you'd work with

Mark Schulz, Founder & Principal

I founded Aequum Labs to bring together the two things I care about most: solving hard healthcare marketing problems, and finding out how AI can change the way we do that work.

I've spent more than 25 years in pharma marketing, from three sides of the table: brand management at Pfizer, commercial strategy and analytics at WebMD/Medscape, and omnichannel experience planning at Omnicom healthcare agencies. The work spans launches and loss of exclusivity, HCP and patient programs, and therapeutic areas from oncology and cardiovascular to HIV, rare disease, and multiple sclerosis.

Along the way I started building AI tools, and led an agency AI program that grew from a 25-person pilot to more than 500 people.
